feat(adjudicant): 113 — read-only Investigations / cases surface on the Adjudicant (Inspector-General) observer plane.

The §B/§G Adjudicant plane OBSERVES / INVESTIGATES / ESCALATES — it never authorizes or weakens. This plan adds the Investigations surface that lets an operator PIVOT from a single audit record (by `intentHash`) into a correlated CASE view, grounded entirely on the existing read-only `audit.query` procedure over the admin SDK's read-only router. The surface authorizes nothing and weakens nothing: it composes pure-read tRPC queries and never touches the four `.mutation` procedures, preserving the router-level write-isolation seam (§C/§D).

- **Pure case correlation (`src/lib/case-correlation.ts`):** `correlateCase(input, seedIntentHash)` builds a correlated case from a window of audit records + a seed hash, purely (no I/O, no clock, no randomness). A "case" is the union of (1) records sharing the seed's `envelope.actor.sessionId` (the stream the 093 chain is also grouped on) and (2) the supersession LINEAGE — the `supersedes.predecessorIntentHash` links walked transitively BACKWARD from the seed (predecessors are pulled in even across sessions) and FORWARD to a fixpoint (successors whose chain reaches the seed). Members are returned in a deterministic timeline order (by `at` asc, ties by `intentHash`), each tagged with WHY it belongs (seed / same_session / lineage_predecessor / lineage_successor) and carrying its index-aligned 092 verify-on-read verdict. It NEVER mutates an input record (members reference the same objects) and makes NO decision — it composes FACTS only (§C, invariant #1).
- **Read-only case hook (`src/hooks/useCase.ts`):** fetches a window via the read-only `audit.query` and runs `correlateCase`. The client is typed against `ReadOnlyAdminRouter`, so the only reachable procedures are `.query`; `tenantScope` is threaded to the host-enforced isolation seam when supplied. Correlation is window-scoped (capped at the SDK's 500) — an investigation SIGNAL over the fetched page, not a global proof.
- **Surface components (`src/components/cases/CaseView.tsx`, `InvestigationsExplorer.tsx`):** the case timeline renders each correlated record with its decision kind, intent kind, timestamp, correlation reason, supersession reason, and a DENY-BY-DEFAULT integrity badge (a tampered record renders a loud tamper badge rather than as authoritative). The explorer is a seed-hash input that opens a case; both are pure presentation of correlated facts.
- **Route + nav (`src/app/cases/page.tsx`, `src/components/shell/Sidebar.tsx`):** new `/cases` route mounting only `.query`-backed hooks; an "Investigations" nav entry on the observer shell.

Per the authoritative human-gate override, this plan's app-wiring re-targets to the NEW `apps/adjudicant` (the write-isolated OBSERVER plane scaffolded by 111, extended by 112) — `apps/console` and `apps/adjutant` are left unchanged, and no write surface is added or moved.

The plan's package-level tasks are NO-OPs already satisfied upstream: T1 (the `replayWithIntegrity` envelope-tamper double-count fix) landed in 111; T2 (threading `tenantScope` into `audit.byHash`'s `getByIntentHash`) and T3 (the postgres reference store accepting `tenantScope` on the by-hash seam) landed in 112. The §6 audit/admin-sdk test updates landed with those plans. No mutation is introduced; the closed 6-outcome `Decision` algebra and `intentHash` recipe are untouched.
